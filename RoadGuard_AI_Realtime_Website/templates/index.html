Custom Asset Requirements

Standard CARLA releases do not ship with Indian cattle meshes or procedural pothole geometries. For high-fidelity visual simulation:

  • Cattle/Dogs: Requires importing custom rigged FBX skeletal meshes into Unreal Engine / CARLA content library.
  • Potholes: Requires decal projection or road mesh displacement mapping.
  • Current Prototype Behavior: Benchmark scenarios proxy animal and pothole obstacle coordinates and bounding boxes realistically to test perception and planner response.
